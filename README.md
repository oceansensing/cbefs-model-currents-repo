# cbefs-model-currents-repo

The Chesapeake Bay Environmental Forecast System's currents, as gridded data
for the oceansensing.org ocean map: at the surface, averaged over the whole
water column, at the bottom and at fixed depths, from a day behind to two days
ahead.

Updated every three hours.

## Sources and credits

The Virginia Institute of Marine Science's Chesapeake Bay Environmental Forecast System (CBEFS). The files' own license: *"These data are freely available for public use.
